# Buttonwillow 13CW — coach brief

Condensed from `buttonwillow-13cw-guide.md` (the sourced research guide, with links and exact quotes) for use as the live coach's context. Advice is paraphrased; tags name the source: JOH = Billy Johnson (MotoIQ, NSX, has low-power notes), BRK = Kevin Burke (Driver Mod, S2000/C7, low-HP notes), TMC = Model 3 track guide, R360/BLZ = Racers360/Blayze coaching videos, KH = Ken Hill coaching video, LM = Miata drivers' lap notes on lapmeta, FTDA = Cup-car tutorial on the Race #1 layout (shares these corners except Sweeper). Most sources are not Miatas; prefer the low-power notes.

Lap order: Sunrise → Off Ramp → (I-5) → Cotton Corners (right, left, right over a blind crest) → Grapevine → Club Corner → Bus Stop → Truck Stop → Riverside → Kink → Phil Hill → (Drag Strip) → Sweeper → Esses → Sunset → front straight. Numbering differs between sources; use names. A driver saying "Turn 2" means Off Ramp; this driver's "Turn 9" means Phil Hill.

## Track-wide themes

- Biggest common gain (BLZ): earlier, smoother brake release in the medium-speed corners — Sunrise, Phil Hill, Sweeper, Sunset (a little at the Bus Stop). Drivers hold peak brake pressure too long and over-slow the entry.
- Most common error across coaches (KH, R360): turning in too early.
- Bumpy track, especially near apexes; the dirt off-track is powdery and can roll a car. If going off, go off straight and slow down before rejoining (JOH, BRK).
- Low-power car rhythm: exits matter most before the longer straights (Off Ramp → I-5, Bus Stop → Riverside, Sunset → front straight). Riverside-to-Phil Hill is the biggest full-throttle section for low-power cars (BRK).

## Sunrise (fast left at the end of the front straight)

- Brake: use the access road / red-white kerb on the right as the reference (JOH, R360). You can brake later than you think (TMC, BRK).
- Turn-in and apex: slow, smooth hands at turn-in (R360, KH). Slightly late apex, about a car length after the start of the inside kerb (JOH, R360). Higher-power sources prefer an earlier apex because the corner is banked — this driver's car is low-power, so default to the later apex.
- Release: the big gain is releasing peak brake pressure sooner to carry more entry speed; don't over-slow (TMC, BLZ).
- Exit: back to throttle about halfway through; a Miata can go to full throttle right after the apex (JOH, BRK, LM). Track out wide, parallel to the exit kerb; the road crests and falls away on exit.
- Priority: entry speed (no long straight follows) per JOH, though the paraphrase source says exit — keep both sensible.

## Off Ramp (left kink, then slow right hairpin; "Turn 2")

- Brake zone is bumpy and passes through the left kink (TMC). Common mistake: turning in too early (R360, BLZ, KH). Turn-in marker for the hairpin: the end of the flag stand on the right (R360, BLZ).
- Line for low-power cars: late turn-in at the left kink, be about mid-road in the brake zone, turn into the hairpin from the middle of the road and let the car track out (JOH, R360). Brake straighter, with a lighter trail past peak so the car rotates later (KH). A Miata driver found holding the inside faster (LM); Burke trails onto the inside kerb.
- Gear: 2nd (Miata on a sister layout; the AI reference uses 2nd too).
- Exit: the most important part — the strongest acceleration on the lap leads onto I-5. Get on throttle before the camber falls away (BRK, JOH). Sacrifice entry speed for the exit; don't over-cook the entry (JOH, TMC).
- Mistakes: diving in and loading the front; too fast an entry washes the car wide into the bumps.

## Cotton Corners (right, left, then right over a blind crest)

- Brake: use the kerb on the left as the reference, braking shortly before it (JOH, TMC). Burke says brake early enough from I-5 to make the turn-in.
- First right: hold the car right a bit longer, later turn-in, apex just past mid-kerb, be off the brakes before the kerb (BLZ, JOH). Don't go to throttle too early after it — that makes the car push in the left (R360). Don't crab toward the inside before turn-in (BLZ).
- Left: a partial sacrifice to set up the last right, but don't over-slow it — about mid-road is the compromise (R360, TMC). Between left and right: hands straight, drive straight briefly, stay mid-track (BLZ, LM).
- Last right over the crest: late apex; it can be turned in early since it's under 90° (JOH, KH). Full throttle before the right-hand kerb (TMC); the car goes light over the crest and will push wide if the setup is wrong.
- Miata note: the inside kerb and dirt are usable; dirt can wash the car out (FTDA, LM).

## Grapevine (right)

- Usually flat or a slight lift in a low-power, high-grip car; a lift tucks the nose in, then straight back to full throttle over the kerb (BRK, TMC). Turn in earlier if you can't reach the apex; the kerb helps rotation (JOH).
- Mistakes: getting on power too soon; it's quick and spins happen (JOH, S2K). Priority: exit. No lift needed on the small rise to Club in a low-power car (BRK).

## Club Corner (right)

- A small lift is all that's needed; a Miata may be flat (TMC). Don't use more than half the apex kerb — it's bumpy and upsets the car (JOH).
- Turn-in: sources disagree on early vs. late; if the car runs wide on exit, turn in later (TMC).
- Exit to the middle of the road to set up the Bus Stop. "Don't be a hero in Club and ruin the Bus Stop" (TMC).

## Bus Stop (fast left bend, then slower kerb-jump section)

- First bend is basically flat (TMC); apex at the light-grey concrete patch (JOH).
- Second part: brake in a straight line, a bit later than most drivers do (BLZ: a car length or so later), avoid the outside kerb on the brakes (JOH; TMC says it's drivable). Release and add a little throttle at turn-in; use the whole inside kerb, left tyres almost in the dirt (JOH).
- Throttle as the car lands over the kerb (BLZ); be ready to catch the rear on landing (BRK). Full throttle at or just after the inside apex (TMC).
- Mistakes: pinching the exit (turn in later), over-slowing the exit (LM). Many go off on the exit. Priority: the exit and the run into Riverside — this corner can ruin a low-power lap (BRK).

## Truck Stop (left)

- Often flat in a car with more grip than power (JOH, BRK). Turn in from mid-road, apex, then get back to the left side to set up Riverside (JOH, TMC).

## Riverside (long fast right, banked, slightly tightening, late second apex)

- Little or no braking — aggressive braking over-slows it (TMC). Stay mid-track until you see the apex kerb (JOH); a power-limited car may hug the inside (BRK). Very bumpy on the extreme inside; the middle is smoother (FTDA, S2000 video).
- Don't lift early anticipating understeer and then hold maintenance throttle — drive it in, lift smoothly only if it pushes, then aggressively back to throttle for the late second apex (R360). Johnson prefers steady maintenance throttle and never a full lift. All agree: late second apex, full throttle before the bump at the apex, use the whole road on exit (TMC, BLZ).
- Priority: mid-corner and exit speed (JOH, BLZ) — the run to Phil Hill is short.

## Kink (fast left after Riverside, bump at the apex)

- Flat in most cars (TMC, BRK, FTDA); a brief lift is acceptable if it buys a faster Riverside exit (JOH, BLZ). Big bump at the apex — relaxed hands. Then move back left for the Phil Hill brake zone.

## Phil Hill (uphill braking, blind crest; "Turn 9")

- Brake uphill in a straight line from the left side, finishing downshifts before turn-in (JOH, FTDA). Uphill gives extra grip: brake deep and late but get the rotation done before the crest (S2000 video). Release the brake earlier and smoother instead of holding peak pressure then snapping off (BLZ).
- Turn-in and apex: apex on the back side of the crest, late apex; but the most common error is turning in too late to reach it (JOH, TMC). Early turn-in leaves you fighting steering lock on exit (KH) — aim to be straight-ish at the crest.
- Kerb: a Miata is better not jumping the apex kerb (LM); it can bottom the car (TMC).
- Exit: back to power as you crest and land; if you're sure of the exit, full throttle from the top (JOH, BRK, FTDA). Overshooting here wrecks cars — if going off, go straight (BRK, JOH).
- Priority: sources disagree (exit vs. late braking); for a low-power car, favour the exit.

## Drag Strip

- Long straight with a flat left kink; get back over to the left for the Sweeper (TMC, BRK).

## Sweeper (long right that tightens; double apex)

- Brake briefly in a straight line on the left (JOH), lighter and a touch earlier than instinct so the car stays settled ("neutral platform"); peak brake pressure at turn-in after the downshift upsets it (BLZ). Brake to or past the first apex (KH).
- Line: treat it as two corners / a double apex (BRK, S2000 video). Hug the inside to about halfway, then track out and turn in late for the second, late apex (JOH). Plan backwards from two-thirds through, where you want to be wide (KH). Track out more after the first apex so throttle comes earlier (LM).
- Exit: the last apex and the full-throttle point are non-negotiable; squeeze to full and track out over the left kerb into the Esses (TMC, JOH).
- Priority: sources disagree (entry speed vs. give up entry for exit). Mistake: staying tight on the inside and losing the exit; overdoing entry gives terminal understeer.

## Esses (L-R-L series)

- Flat in a low-power car (BRK, TMC). Late-apex every bend (hit the back side), especially the last left, which is quick and where early turn-ins happen (JOH). Kerbs get bigger further along; run the first, only touch the rest.
- If wheels drop at the last left, go off straight to the right and slow before rejoining — a dangerous spot (JOH, BRK, SV coach).

## Sunset (last corner, banked; onto the front straight)

- One of the most important corners: it makes or breaks the front straight in a low-power car (JOH, S2K).
- Brake: kerb on the right as reference (JOH) or before the flag station on the left (TMC); keep the right wheels off the kerb under braking or the car pitches (S2000 video). Don't hustle in with too much entry speed — it's slower than it looks (JOH, TMC).
- Brake technique: delay the downshift; release toward lighter pressure held longer (BLZ).
- Apex very late; pick up power just before the apex and squeeze to full throttle (JOH, TMC). There is a dip at the apex: keep the wheels out of it (BRK) but time the first throttle as the car reaches it (BLZ).
- Mistakes: pinching the exit, attacking the inside kerb too hard. Priority: exit speed.
