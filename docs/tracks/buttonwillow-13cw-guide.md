# Buttonwillow Raceway Park — 13CW (Race #13 clockwise): corner-by-corner guide

Compiled 2026-11-15 for the AI driving-coach project. Target car class: NA Miata on street tyres.
Every claim carries a source link. Quotes are exact, under 15 words, and checked against the fetched page text.
Anything that is my own reasoning is labelled **Inference (mine)**. Where nothing was found it says **no source found**.

## 0. How to read this guide

- **Most sources are not Miata guides.** Johnson drove an NSX, Burke an S2000 and a C7 Corvette, and the Model 3 guide is an EV. Where a source says something about low-power or lower-grip cars, I say so. Miata-specific sources are marked **[Miata]**.
- **Not 13CW.** The 2007 Nerd Expo blog ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)) and the 2005 PCA SDR thread ([PCA05](https://forum.pcasdr.org/viewtopic.php?p=6682)) describe other configurations (Race #1). I use them only for corners the layouts share, and flag them.
- **Trackpedia is not independent.** Its car guide ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)) is largely a rewording of the Johnson article. Treat agreement between TP and JOH as one source, not two.
- **Video claims come from YouTube auto-captions** (pulled 2026-11-15; text only, no video downloaded). Auto-captions mis-hear words ("break" for "brake", "curve" for "curb"), so quotes keep the caption's own spelling and each carries a timestamp link to check by ear. Corners in the coaching videos are often identified by sequence rather than name; where I had to infer which corner is meant, I say so.
- **Lapmeta turn labels** such as "Turn 2A" or "Turn 23" are generated by lapmeta and are not defined on the pages I read. I use them only as raw numbers, and any mapping to a named corner is marked as inference.

## 1. Corner list and numbering

### 1.1 What the official map shows

The official map ([MAP](https://buttonwillowraceway.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/brp-track-map-race-13.pdf)) **has no corner numbers**. It only names corners. It does not mark start/finish or direction arrows. It is drawn south-up (the compass rose has S at the top).

- Names on the 13CW route (drawn in red): Sunrise, Off Ramp, I-5, Cotton Corners, Grapevine, Club Corner, Bus Stop, Truck Stop, Riverside, Phil Hill, Drag Strip, Sweeper, Esses, Sunset.
- Names on grey, non-13CW segments: Dog Leg, Atlantic, Pacific, Zorros Corners, Star Mazda Turn, A-Section. (This is my reading of the rendered PDF. The text layer gives labels but not colours.)
- "Kink" (after Riverside) is not labelled on the map, but sources use it.

### 1.2 Lap order and numbers by source

Lap order follows the sources ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4) and [TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/)) and matches the map geometry as I read it. Burke places the finish line after Sunset: “leading to the front straight and finish line” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).

- **Is Phil Hill "Turn 9"?** Only in the TMC scheme. In the JOH scheme Turn 9 is the Bus Stop and Phil Hill is Turn 13. All three numbered sources agree that Turn 2 is Off Ramp.
- **TMC says it uses numbers from Wikipedia.** The Wikipedia article text I fetched has no numbered corner list. Its infobox lists 23 turns, 3.000 mi, for Race #13 CW ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park)). I could not verify the TMC claim.
- **Lapmeta uses a third scheme** with up to Turn 23 and letter suffixes (for example 2A, 2B, 11B, 17C). It is undefined on the pages I read.
- **Name variants:** Off-Ramp / Offramp / Buttonhook(s); Lost Hill / Lost Hills / Phil Hill / Magic Mountain / "Philhill"; Cotton Corner(s); Grape Vine / Grapevine; Bus-Stop / Busstop; Truckstop.
- **Inference (mine):** the coach should reason in names, using this table to translate any number the driver says. "Turn 9" means Phil Hill under the TMC numbering but the Bus Stop under the JOH numbering.

## 2. Corner-by-corner

Format: approach/braking reference · gear · turn-in · apex · exit · common mistakes · priority.

### Sunrise (JOH T1 · TMC 1)

- **Approach/braking:**
  - Brake on the front straight using the access road and the red-and-white kerb on the right as a reference: “use the access road and the red & white curbing on the right” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
  - Alternative reference: brake “right where the other track layout joins the straight away” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=590s)).
  - You can brake later than you think: “you can brake a fair bit later than you think you can” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=590s)); “brake as late as your car can” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Gear (NA Miata):** no source found.
- **Turn-in/apex:** a slightly later apex — “get down to a slightly later apex” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). TMC puts the apex where the inside kerb ends: “right where the inside curbing ends” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=590s)).
- **Exit:** “pick up the throttle half way through the corner” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). Burke says slower cars can go to full throttle earlier: “In slower momentum cars, you are usually able to increase throttle to 100%” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). **[Miata]** An NC driver's note: “need to throttle out right after the apex” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)).
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Over-slowing: “easy to over-slow the car here” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=590s)).
  - Too clean an entry: “Entering at a slow speed while trying to maintain throttle throughout” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).
  - Running wide on exit: “tighter on the inside clipping point” would have avoided it ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).
- **Priority:** carry entry speed — “no long straight after it, entry speed is good” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). Trackpedia disagrees (§6).
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):**
  - Brake reference matches Johnson's access road: “waiting just about this access road” ([R360C 1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmUV0nxZ-g&t=101s)).
  - Apex: “I want to only Apex about a car length after that” (after the start of the apex kerb) ([R360C 1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmUV0nxZ-g&t=117s)). Slow hands at turn-in: “I really want you to slow down your hands” ([R360C 2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmUV0nxZ-g&t=148s)); Ken Hill gives the same note, “slow the initial wheel down just a little bit” ([KH 3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=220s)).
  - Brake release is the big gain: the BLZ coach sees drivers “hold Peak brake pressure for too long” ([BLZ 5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=310s)), so “you're over slowing the corner entry” ([BLZ 6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=403s)). His car's minimum speed: “we might be at 65 66 miles an hour here” ([BLZ 7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=433s)).
  - Exit: get wide and parallel with the exit kerb earlier, “closer to about the middle of the curve” (caption's word for curb) ([R360C 3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmUV0nxZ-g&t=211s)); BLZ uses the point where the rubbered line meets the exit kerb ([BLZ 2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=140s)).
  - **[640 hp Viper]** “an early apex line into turn one” ([WRR 0:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcuztI-PmiY&t=13s)) — conflicts with the late-apex advice (§6).

### Off Ramp (JOH T2 · TMC 2)

Left kink, then a slow right hairpin. The shape is “A short, fast curve directly followed by a moderate, opposite hairpin” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).

- **Approach/braking:** Burke begins braking at the kink: “I personally begin braking at the kink and trail into the corner” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). TMC notes the braking zone is “over a very bumpy surface” and passes through a kink ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=600s)).
- **Gear:** **[Miata, but Race #1 CW, 2007]** “Brake hard and down shift to 2nd.” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)). A Porsche driver on the *counter-clockwise* configuration: “It's still a 2nd gear turn” ([PCA05](https://forum.pcasdr.org/viewtopic.php?p=6682)). No 13CW-specific gear source found.
- **Turn-in:** late for the left kink — “Turn in a bit late for the left kink once you can see daylight” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
- **Apex/line, by car type:**
  - Lower power: “turn into the right hand hairpin from the middle of the road”, then let the car track out ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
  - Higher power: “keeping a tighter line is faster” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
  - Terrain: the apex is “on-camber/banked but the road falls away the further you track out” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). TMC: “straddle them under the center of the car” (the concrete patches) ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=600s)).
  - Burke uses the inside kerb: “placing the car on the inside curbing” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). He adds that some cars “may need to move left as they brake” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - **[Miata]** An NC driver found inside faster: “probably not a late apex corner” and “holding the inside seems to be a lot faster” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)).
- **Exit:** “the exit is far more important than the entry” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). Burke: strongest acceleration on the lap, “the highest longitudinal G force acceleration of anywhere on the track” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). Get on power before the camber changes: “get on throttle before the camber changes” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Common mistakes:**
  - “adopt a faster entry line” (taking the kink straight, then a wide hairpin) ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).
  - Over-cooking the entry: “not over-cook the entry” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=600s)).
  - **[Miata]** “diving in seems to transfer too much weigh to the fronts” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)); “a missed opportunity for a wider line into Buttonhook” ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
- **Speed data (lapmeta, auto-generated)** — see §4 caveat:
  - NC Miata: “braked from 131 to 57 kph in 84 m for Turn 2A” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)).
  - Spec Miata NB1: “braked from 131 to 65 kph in 80 m for Turn 2A” ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
  - **Inference (mine):** "Turn 2A" is the Off Ramp hairpin. It is the first heavy braking zone after Sunrise and matches the description, but lapmeta does not define the label.
- **Priority:** exit over entry. Johnson, Trackpedia and the Model 3 guide all say this.
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):**
  - The most common Turn 2 mistake per Racers360: “what we typically see is drivers starting to turn in too early” ([R360T3 0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=16s)). Visual reference: “a great visual is going to be this flag stand” on the right ([R360T3 0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=16s)); turn in at “our driver at the end of this flag stand” ([R360T3 0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=47s)), which gives a straighter brake zone so “they can break with straighter hands” ([R360T3 1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=63s)).
  - The BLZ coach gives the same marker independently: “flag stand on the right hand side”, and says you then “carry Full Throttle deeper into turn two” ([BLZ 7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=449s), [BLZ 7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=465s)).
  - **Lower-power cars:** Dion holds a high-power car further left, but for “lower horsepower cars your spec miatas” he says “I want to be about middle of the road” in the brake zone ([R360T3 1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=110s)). This matches Johnson's low-power line (hairpin from mid-road).
  - Ken Hill: “I like to see you come in here a little bit straighter” ([KH 4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=266s)), with peak braking before the apex and a lighter trail past it so the car rotates later and points straighter — “faster in and faster out is the idea” ([KH 5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=358s)). Inference (mine): Ken's "coming down here in the tube" at 3:55 is caption noise for Turn 2; the drawing that follows is the Off Ramp.
  - **[640 hp Viper]** “setting up for a late apex into off ramp” ([WRR 0:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcuztI-PmiY&t=28s)).

### I-5 (straight after Off Ramp)

- Burke: “I-5 is a decently long straight after the exit of Off Ramp” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- Gear, braking point: no source found beyond the Cotton Corners entry notes below.

### Cotton Corners (JOH T3–T5 · TMC 3)

Right, left, right (the last over a blind crest).

- **Approach/braking:**
  - “Use the curbing on the left as a braking reference point” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
  - “braking about 40-50ft before the curbing” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=614s)).
  - Burke wants earlier braking: “brake early from I-5 so you can make the turn in” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “Slow down a lot, and turn at the end of the left berm.” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Gear:** no source found.
- **Turn-in/apex:**
  - Be off the brakes before the right-hand kerb: “get off the brakes and transfer the weight off the front of the car” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
  - “Emphasize a late apex for the series of corners.” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
  - Between corners: “A brief throttle application then tap of the brakes to set the nose” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). Burke instead uses the left foot: “left foot brake a bit to get the car to turn in” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - The last, blind corner: “late apex this blind corner” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)).
- **Exit:** TMC treats the left as a sacrifice to set up the last right: “The left hand corner is a sacrifice in a sense” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=614s)), then “you should be at full throttle” before the right-hand kerb ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=614s)). **[Miata]** Line check from an NC driver: “should be middle of track after the left hand” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)).
- **Common mistakes:** **[Miata]** “washing out in the cotton corners due to dirt” ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
- **Priority:** Burke: “the only thing that matters, is the entry and exit” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). Johnson: “a lot of entry speed can be carried into it” ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). See §6 for the tension between early braking and carrying speed.
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):**
  - Racers360 mistake #2 is “drivers getting to throttle too early” after the first right, which makes the car understeer left; “be patient with your throttle” ([R360T3 2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=155s)) and trail-brake to or slightly past the apex to hold the car right ([R360T3 3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=187s)).
  - Then don't over-sacrifice the left: drivers are “sacrificing too much speed in this left hander” ([R360T3 3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=218s)); “right about mid-road it seems to be the right compromise” ([R360T3 4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=249s)).
  - BLZ: “sacrifice a little bit of speed hold it more right” on the first corner ([BLZ 8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=480s)), later turn-in, “Apex just passed mid curb” ([BLZ 9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=542s)), and “make sure we're not crabbing” toward the inside before turn-in ([BLZ 9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=557s)). Between the left and the last right: “get your hands straight drive it just straight for a bit” and “keep it mid-track” ([BLZ 10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=604s), [BLZ 10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=619s)).
  - Ken Hill: a better turn-in point is “as you approach the end of the curb” ([KH 7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=456s)); over the crest (less than 90°) “we can turn it early there” ([KH 8:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=535s)).
  - **[640 hp Viper]** “a late apex in the cotton corners” ([WRR 0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcuztI-PmiY&t=44s)).

### Grapevine (JOH T6 · TMC 4)

- **Approach/braking:** none described by Johnson. Trackpedia's unnamed "Turn 6" (the number Johnson gives Grapevine) says it can usually be flat: “it can usually be taken flat out” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)). Burke and TMC describe a lift (below).
- **Gear:** **[S2000]** the driver in the S2KI analysis “short shifts before exiting the Grapevine” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)). No Miata gear source found.
- **Turn-in/apex:** get to the apex, using kerb: “If you are having trouble getting down to the apex, turn in earlier.” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)); “The curbing can help rotate the most understeering of cars” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). S2KI: “the fastest line is to be all over the curbing” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).
- **Exit:** TMC: “a lift off of the power to get the front end to tuck in”, then “right back to full power over the curb on Grape Vine” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=614s)). Burke, for low-HP, high-grip cars: “there is a slight lift at Grapevine that may be required” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)), then “it is important to get on throttle quickly” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Common mistakes:** Johnson: “this is a pretty quick corner and spins do happen here” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). S2KI: “all too easy to want to get on the power too soon” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).
- **Priority:** exit — Burke says the Grapevine-to-Club section is “all about the exit” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Straight to Club Corner:** “in lower horsepower cars, it is not necessary to lift” on the small rise ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).

### Club Corner (JOH T7 · TMC 5)

- **Approach/braking:** TMC: only a small lift — “A small lift is all that is needed on the entry to Corner 5” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=631s)). **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “the Miata is full throttle here” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)), with a slight lift “before or after the turn” to get back right for the Bus Stop ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Gear:** no source found.
- **Turn-in:** Johnson wants quicker hands: “quicker hands at turn-in can help the car” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke wants it early: “turn in nice and early, anticipate any under-steer your car may exhibit” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). TMC warns against too early: “Turn in too early and you'll go too wide on the exit.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=631s)). See §6.
- **Apex:** “using more than half of the apex curbing” upsets the car ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2), sentence begins "I don't like"). Trackpedia notes the inside kerb is “very bumpy” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).
- **Exit:** set up the Bus Stop — “don't be a hero in Club Corner and ruin the Bus Stop” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=631s)). Johnson: “stay in the middle of the road setting up for the next bend” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)).
- **Priority:** keep the car placed for the Bus Stop; no source asks for max entry speed here.

### Bus Stop (JOH T8 + T9 · TMC 6)

Two parts: a fast first bend (Johnson calls T8 “the next bend to the left”, [JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)), then a slower kerb-jumping second part (T9). I did not find a clear statement of the second part's direction. TMC says “The first corner of the bus stop is basically flat out” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=634s)), and the second part is much slower.

- **Approach/braking (second part):**
  - “Brake in a straight line” and release the brakes at turn-in ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)).
  - Burke: “Brake early enough to get the car rotated” so you can be on throttle before the kerb jump ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - Outside-kerb handling differs by source (§6): “You can drive over the curbing while braking.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=634s)) versus “I don't like hitting the curb on the right entering the bus-stop” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)).
  - **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “Be careful not to touch the outside berm when braking.” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Gear:** no source found.
- **Turn-in:** on a little throttle — “release the brakes and add a little throttle at turn-in” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke: “get on throttle at the green arrow” (a point on his diagram), keeping throttle over the kerb ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Apex:** the first bend's apex is “where the light grey concrete patch/apex curbing is” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). For the second part: “Use the entire curb where your left tires are almost in the dirt.” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Trackpedia: “Do not be afraid of cutting the corner” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).
- **Exit:** “at full throttle at the inside apex or just a moment after it” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=634s)). Burke: “be prepared to catch the rear end on the landing” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “This is full throttle for the Miata until Riverside.” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Pinching the exit: Johnson says “pinching off the exit, turn in later” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). TMC says “many people go off on the exit” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=634s)).
  - **[Miata]** “overslowing the exit from Busstop” ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
- **Priority:** the exit and the run into Riverside. Burke, for low-power cars: “this corner has the potential to ruin your lap” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):** the BLZ coach says the driver brakes a tick early: “you could probably break a good car length car length and a half later” ([BLZ 11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=682s)). Throttle timing: “pick up the throttle as my car lands on the other side” of the kerb ([BLZ 11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=682s)).

### Truck Stop (JOH T10)

- **Approach/braking:** none needed in many cars: “This corner can be flat in many cars” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke: “Truck Stop is typically flat out on a car with Grip>power” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Gear:** **[C7 Corvette]** Burke short-shifts here: “short shift a bit to maintain momentum” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). No Miata gear source found.
- **Turn-in/apex/exit:** “Turn-in from the middle of the road, apex, then track out to the left.” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). TMC: “get back over to the left side of the track just after it” to set up Riverside ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=634s)).
- **Common mistakes, priority:** no source found beyond the above.

### Riverside (JOH T11 · TMC 8)

Long, fast, right-hand, banked. “Riverside has a slight on-camber to it and is a slightly decreasing-radius corner” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke calls it an “extremely fast banked right hand turn that has a deceptively late apex” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).

- **Approach/braking:** little or none. TMC: “use a lot less braking than you think” and “Aggressive braking here will over-slow the car.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=647s)). Trackpedia: “Do a bit of braking beforehand” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)). **[Miata]** a Spec Miata driver lists “the potential for later braking into Riverside for a wider and faster entry” as an improvement ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
- **Gear:** no source found. (Burke says Riverside is the biggest full-throttle section for low-power cars — see Priority.)
- **Turn-in/apex:** Johnson: “stay mid-track until you can see the apex curbing” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke, for power-limited cars: “you may be able to hug the inside of this turn completely” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “Stay around the middle of the track and look for the exit apex.” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Exit:** Johnson: “have some maintenance throttle the entire time through the corner” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). TMC: “There is a big bump right at the apex” and wants “full throttle before the bump” with relaxed hands ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=647s)).
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Lifting: “Lifting off the throttle causes engine-braking that slows the car down significantly” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke: “don't lift completely” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - Trackpedia warns that lifting in the corner “will prove catastrophic” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).
  - **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “I have to lift slightly to get the front pointed to the apex” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Priority:** mid-corner speed, not entry speed — “The key to this corner is mid-corner speed.” ([JOH p2](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/2)). Burke: for low-power cars the Bus Stop exit leads into “the largest full throttle section at Buttonwillow for low horsepower cars” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Video coaching (auto-captions) — these disagree with Johnson's maintenance-throttle advice (§6):**
  - Racers360 mistake #3: drivers are “anticipating that understeer so they lift early”, then hold maintenance throttle, “artificially lowering the ceiling of grip the car has” ([R360T3 4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=279s), [R360T3 4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=295s)). Instead: “don't anticipate the understeer drive it hard in”, lift smoothly when it starts to understeer, then get aggressively back to throttle for the second apex ([R360T3 4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=295s)–[R360T3 5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rN4BBbop8hU&t=325s)).
  - BLZ: the driver does the first half well but “we over sacrifice the second half of it” ([BLZ 11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=697s)); a later second apex, and “would much rather see you back to full throttle” using all the road ([BLZ 12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=729s)), “even if that means you need a tiny lift to this left-hander” (the Kink) ([BLZ 12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=744s)), because “there's not much of a straightaway here into Phil Hill” ([BLZ 12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=760s)).
  - Ken Hill calls his method “a little bit car specific”: come in “to have my wheel straight and I accelerate in”, with “a little bit of breaks down in here to help the rotation” ([KH 12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=776s), [KH 13:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=791s), [KH 13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=807s)).
  - **[640 hp Viper]** “touch on the brakes in the river side”, car middle-left, late apex ([WRR 1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcuztI-PmiY&t=75s)).

### Kink (JOH T12; unlabelled on MAP)

Fast left kink after Riverside.

- **Approach/braking:** “A brief lift in the throttle right before turn-in” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). Johnson places you mid-to-right beforehand (“middle to right side of the road”) ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).
- **Gear:** no source found.
- **Apex/exit:** “a bump just after the apex that can be unnerving” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)); “a massive bump at the apex of this kink” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=647s)); “This kink should be easily flat out.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=647s)). Burke also says flat in most cars (“That kink should be flat out in most cars.”) ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Fastest corner?** A 2016 NASA note calls it “the fastest turn when going clockwise at Buttonwillow Raceway” ([NASA16](https://nasaspeed.news/columns/move-of-the-month/how-to-avoid-a-disabled-car-at-110mph-at-buttonwillow-raceway)). Johnson says Riverside is (§6).
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):** BLZ accepts “a tiny lift to this left-hander” if Riverside's exit is faster ([BLZ 12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=744s)); **[640 hp Viper]** “a little lift for this kink” ([WRR 1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcuztI-PmiY&t=75s)).
- **Common mistakes, priority:** no source found.

### Phil Hill (JOH T13 "Lost Hill/Phil Hill/Magic Mountain" · TMC 9)

Fast, blind, over a crest. TMC calls it “arguably one of the most challenging corners on the track” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)).

- **Approach/braking:**
  - Burke: Riverside's exit feeds “a relatively long straight that leads to a uphill braking zone” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - Johnson: be on the left, “brake in a straight line and finish all downshifts before turning in” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).
  - TMC: “brake in a straight line pointed at the outside curbing” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)).
  - Burke wants you on the right: “give up some speed, and get to the right side of the track” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). See §6.
- **Gear:** Johnson: finish downshifts before turn-in (above). Gear number: no source found.
- **Turn-in:** Johnson: “turn-in early to make sure you get down to the apex” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). Burke: “turn in just late enough to line up the exit in a straight line” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “Slow down a lot and use a really late apex.” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Apex:** late — “Emphasize a later apex” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)); TMC: “apexing on the back-side of it” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)).
- **Kerb at the apex:** Johnson uses a little kerb and lands “for a perfect track-out” (his words: “launches the car in the air a bit”) ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). TMC says riding the kerb may bottom the car (“may cause the car to bottom out”) ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)). S2KI: “takes a gentle, but quick hop through the corner on light throttle” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)). **[Miata]** a Spec Miata driver lists “not jumping Philhill” as an improvement ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
- **Exit:** “Get back to power as you are cresting the hill.” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)); “get on throttle just after the crest of the hill” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)); “gently add some steering input and power” after landing ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)). **[Miata]** an NC driver's note: “Need to track more to the right for Phil Hill.” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)); the note does not say which part of the corner.
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Turning in too late: “The most common error for this corner is turning in too late” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).
  - Apexing at the crest: “not apexing at the horizon” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)).
  - Overshooting: “famous for people overshooting and destroying expensive parts” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - If you are going off, Johnson says “drive off straight” and bring the car back later ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).
- **Priority (sources disagree):** Johnson: “getting back to power is more important than carrying a lot of entry speed” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). TMC: “better to be conservative on the exit” and “focus on braking late on the entry” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)). See §6.
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):**
  - Approach: “this is Lost Hills”, “start all the way on the left side” and “break on this hill” ([SVJR 2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9amfL-GG-0s&t=144s)) — agrees with Johnson's left-side setup.
  - BLZ: “we just need to work on that break release happening earlier” ([BLZ 13:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=822s)); the driver holds peak pressure too long, then snaps off the brake.
  - Ken Hill: “when we come over the top here again early turn in” ([KH 14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=854s)) leaves the driver fighting steering angle on the exit; he used to set up passes into the Sweeper because “I'd find people holding wheel after the apex” ([KH 14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=870s)). Inference (mine): "over the top" just before the Sweeper is Phil Hill; Ken does not name it.

### Drag Strip (straight) and the kink before Sweeper

- **Approach to Sweeper:** Burke: “lead you down another long straight, just after a left kink” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). TMC: “Get back over to the left for” Sweeper ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=660s)); Burke says the kink is flat in most cars.
- **Johnson's T14:** the article skips from T13 to T15. **Inference (mine):** T14 is probably this kink, but the article does not say so.
- **Gear, braking point:** no source found for the straight itself. For lapmeta speed data see §4.

### Sweeper (JOH T15 · TMC 10)

A long, tightening corner at the end of the Drag Strip, leading into the Esses. Direction: Burke calls it a “sweeping right turn” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)), which matches the map geometry as I read it; TMC calls it a left-hander (§6). Johnson says it is “one of the harder corners on the track to do it fast” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).

- **Approach/braking:**
  - Johnson: “Brake briefly in a straight line on the left side of the road” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).
  - Burke: “use the width of the corner as a braking zone” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
  - S2KI: “brakes late, and dodges the urge to stay tight, inside the Sweeper” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).
  - TMC: “brake in a straight line and carry a lot of speed” to the first apex ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=672s)).
- **Gear:** no source found.
- **Turn-in/apex:** Johnson: hug the inside to about halfway (“Hug the inside of the road until roughly half way through the corner”), then track out and “be patient and turn-in later for a late apex” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). Burke: “in fact a double apex” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). TMC: a "diamond" approach — “the approach to the corner feels like a diamond” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=672s)). **[Miata]** NC driver: “track out more after the first apex” so the car can roll onto throttle earlier ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)).
- **Exit:** “squeeze the power to full throttle” and “Track out up and over the entire curbing on the left into the esses.” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). TMC: “The last apex and full throttle point are non negotiable.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=672s)). S2KI: tracks out “all the way to the edge of the track” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).
- **Common mistakes:** Trackpedia: “exaggerate and you will get terminal understeer” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)). TMC: staying tight inside costs the exit (S2KI above).
- **Priority (sources disagree):** Johnson: “The key to do this corner fast is entryspeed, entryspeed, entryspeed.” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). Burke: “giving up a little bit of entry speed pays extremely large dividends” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). See §6.
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):**
  - BLZ: an entry-speed corner where “I want a neutral platform” ([BLZ 14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=884s)); the driver's peak brake pressure comes after the downshift, at turn-in, which upsets the car. Fix: “a slightly earlier and lighter brake Zone” and work off the brakes ([BLZ 15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=930s)); “think neutral platform think lighter breaks” ([BLZ 16:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=961s)).
  - Ken Hill: plan backwards from “two-thirds of the way through the sweeper”, where you want to be wide for the exit ([KH 15:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=901s)); “a low entry into the beginning of the sweeper puts you out wide” ([KH 15:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=916s)), so enter lower and straighter and accelerate out toward the three-quarter point. “it's a big entry corner”: brake to or past the apex ([KH 16:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=997s)).
  - **[640 hp Viper]** drifts out to the left before the apex, then early throttle ([WRR 1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcuztI-PmiY&t=106s)).

### Esses (JOH T16–T20 · TMC 11)

- **Approach/braking:** none — it is a flat-out series for lower-power cars. Burke: “The Esses can be taken flat in a low horsepower car, relatively drama free” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). TMC: “These are easily all flat out.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=684s)).
- **Gear:** **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “My Miata is running ~6000 RPM in 3rd here” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)). No 13CW-specific gear source found.
- **Line:** late-apex every corner: “late-apex (hit the back side) of every corner through the esses” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). Set up the last left from the right: “get over the curb and stay tight to the right” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=684s)).
- **Kerbs:** Burke: “the curbs get larger the further you go down the straight” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** run over the first kerb, then only touch the others ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)); the same blog says “accelerate all the way though here” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
- **Exit:** the last left is the sensitive one — “The last turn in the esses (left hand Turn 20) is very quick” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). Burke notes the last kink may push some fast cars off to the right.
- **Common mistakes:** “you turned in too early for 20” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). Off-track recovery: “Keep the steering wheel straight” and “it is perfectly fine to drive with two tires in the dirt” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). Burke: “its important to go off in a straight line” and warns of “a large rollover risk” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Priority:** late apexes so the exit of the last left comes up cleanly (Johnson, above).
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):** the Speed Ventures instructor warns that at the end of the Esses, if you go off to the right you must slow down before rejoining, calling it a dangerous spot ([SVJR 6:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9amfL-GG-0s&t=406s)–[SVJR 7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9amfL-GG-0s&t=422s)).

### Sunset (JOH T21 · TMC 12) — last corner, onto the front straight

“Sunset is banked/on-camber and is one of the most important corners on the track” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). **[S2000, but about low power]** “makes or breaks the run down the front straight” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)), “especially in a lower power car” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).

- **Approach/braking:**
  - Johnson: “Use the curbing on the right as a brake reference point” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)).
  - TMC uses a different cue: “The braking happens before the flagging station on the left.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=694s)). It also says “There is also no braking indication” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=694s)).
  - Burke: “stay calm, brake early and throttle at the apex, nice and smooth” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Gear:** **[Miata, Race #1 CW, 2007]** “about 4000 RPM in 4th before DS to 3” while still braking ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)). **[S2000]** Burke's own S2000 laps involve third gear here: he describes a missed gear on the last turn, “went to grab 3rd but shifter went into 5th by mistake” ([YT G1dxF9LBZkw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G1dxF9LBZkw)), and “miss third gear” in the article ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). No 13CW Miata gear source found.
- **Turn-in/apex:** Johnson: “trail off the brakes down to a slightly late apex” and “Pick up power right before the apex” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). TMC: “The apex is extremely late here.” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=694s)).
- **Exit:** “squeeze to full throttle” through the apex, using the exit kerb onto the straight ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)). Burke warns about a dip at the apex: “There is a large dip at the apex of sunset” and “9/10 its best to stay out of it” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). Johnson uses the exit kerb, so the sources differ.
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Entry speed: “not hustle the car into this corner with too much entry speed” ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)); “you expect it to be so fast” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=694s)).
  - Forcing the exit: “if you force the car too hard on the exit and pinch it” the exit speed is worse ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/) · [video 11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6Gm_Xv3f3Y&t=694s)).
  - Attacking the inside kerb: “dodging a common mistake of attacking the inside curbing too aggressively” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)).
  - Overshooting: Burke: “if you overshoot this corner, go off in a straight line” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
- **Priority:** exit. Johnson ties it to Off Ramp: “Like Turn 2, it is important to not hustle the car into this corner” (his sentence continues with the exit) ([JOH p4](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/4)).
- **Video coaching (auto-captions):** BLZ gives Sunset the same note as Turn 1 — peak brake pressure comes too late, after the downshift: “you downshift too early let's delay that downshift” ([BLZ 17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=1038s)), then release toward much lighter pressure held longer ([BLZ 16:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=1007s)). Throttle timing uses the apex dip: “wait until I get the car in this dip” and “this is my initial throttle application” ([BLZ 16:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=1007s)). Burke says to keep the car out of that dip, so see §6.

### Not on 13CW

Dog Leg, Atlantic, Pacific, Zorros Corners, Star Mazda Turn and A-Section appear on the map on grey segments ([MAP](https://buttonwillowraceway.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/brp-track-map-race-13.pdf)). No advice collected. Note that Johnson's Sweeper corner is a different corner from the Star Mazda turn ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html) covers the Star Mazda version of the layout, Race #1).

### General track character (all corners)

- Bumps near apexes: “a fairly flat, high-speed track with a lot of aggressive bumps” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/)).
- Off-track: “powdery roll-inducing dirt” — avoid going off sideways ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)).
- Dirt on the line affects the track state: “the track was dirty, which affected their line” ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
- Where the lap time is (BLZ): the biggest gain is entry speed through earlier, smoother brake release in the “more medium speed sweeper style corners” — Turn 1, Sunset, Phil Hill and the Sweeper, a little at the Bus Stop ([BLZ 0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=46s), [BLZ 17:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg&t=1053s)).
- Common across coaches: early turn-in. Ken Hill's overall note is “overall was a lot of early turn ins” ([KH 2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAFOd0VnvyI&t=124s)); the BLZ uploader's own summary also mentions early turn-in ([YT 9J73FGItDJg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9J73FGItDJg)).

## 6. Conflicts between sources

1. **Corner numbering.** Three schemes (JOH 1–21, TMC 1–12, lapmeta up to 23 with letters), plus Trackpedia's own slips: it lists "Turn 12" twice (Kink and Lost Hill) and labels Turn 21 "Sunrise" while describing the corner before the last straight ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)). "Turn 9" is Phil Hill in TMC but the Bus Stop in JOH. Only "Turn 2 = Off Ramp" is shared.
2. **Corner order in S2KI.** The S2KI text goes Grapevine → “heading toward Cotton Corner” ([S2K](https://s2ki.com/?p=10653)), the reverse of the MAP, JOH and TMC order.
3. **Off Ramp line.** Johnson/Trackpedia: late turn-in at the left kink, and for lower-power cars enter the hairpin from mid-road and track out wide ([JOH p1](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw)). Burke: brake at the kink and trail onto the inside kerb ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). An NC Miata driver: “holding the inside seems to be a lot faster” ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)). The 2007 Miata blog offered two lines: late apex on the left kink, or an early apex and a double apex ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)).
4. **Phil Hill: what to prioritise.** Johnson says exit and power matter more than entry speed ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). TMC says be conservative on exit and focus on braking late ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/)). Burke sides with an exit-straight turn-in ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)).
5. **Phil Hill: turn-in timing.** Johnson: “turn-in early to make sure you get down to the apex” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)), and the most common error is turning in too late. Burke: “turn in just late enough to line up the exit in a straight line” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). The 2007 Miata blog: “use a really late apex” ([NX07](https://nerdexpo.blogspot.com/2007/10/buttonwillow-raceway-race-configuration.html)). Johnson asks for an early turn-in and a late apex together; the other two texts speak mainly about the apex.
6. **Phil Hill: side of the track on approach.** Johnson: be on the left. Burke: get to the right. TMC: an angle, not fully left, braking toward the outside kerb. Burke's passage may describe a different point in the approach.
7. **Phil Hill: kerb.** Johnson uses the apex kerb and gets airborne. TMC warns it may bottom the car. A Spec Miata driver lists "not jumping Philhill" as a lap-time improvement ([LM 38941](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/38941)).
8. **Sweeper: entry versus exit.** Johnson: “entryspeed, entryspeed, entryspeed” ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)). Burke: give up entry speed for exit ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)). An NC driver says track out more after the first apex ([LM 16819](https://lapmeta.com/en/lap/detail/16819)). Possibly reconcilable by braking late but still prioritising the exit throttle point.
9. **Grapevine: lift or flat.** Trackpedia: usually flat. Burke and TMC: a lift ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw), [TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/)). Johnson: smooth inputs only.
10. **Club Corner: turn-in.** Johnson and Burke ask for an early or quick turn-in; TMC warns early turn-in goes wide ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/)).
11. **Bus Stop: outside kerb on entry.** TMC: you can drive over the curbing while braking. Johnson and the 2007 Miata blog: avoid it.
12. **Cotton Corners: speed versus early braking.** TMC: carry maximum entry speed (brake 40–50 ft before the kerb). Burke: brake early so you do not track out.
13. **Fastest corner.** Johnson and Trackpedia: Riverside. A 2016 NASA note: the Kink ([NASA16](https://nasaspeed.news/columns/move-of-the-month/how-to-avoid-a-disabled-car-at-110mph-at-buttonwillow-raceway)).
14. **Riverside: entry speed.** TMC: enter faster, brush the brakes. Johnson: mid-corner speed matters more than a hustled entry; he says it may feel faster to hustle in with a lot of entry speed but costs more mid-corner.
15. **Sunrise: priority.** Johnson says entry speed is good because no long straight follows. Trackpedia, which paraphrases Johnson, says the opposite: “the exit speed still takes precedence” ([TP](http://trackpedia.racetrackdriving.com/wiki/Turn_by_turn_guide_to_the_Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park_track)).
16. **Sunset: brake reference and kerb.** Johnson: kerb on the right for braking, use the exit kerb. TMC: flagging station on the left. Burke: stay out of the apex dip.
17. **Configuration mix-ups.** Several sources describe Race #1 CW or Race #1 CCW (NX07, PCA05). Do not treat their corner notes as 13CW unless the corner is shared.
18. **Track length.** Wikipedia's infobox: 3.000 mi. Lapmeta: 4.43 km (about 2.75 mi). A Track Night in America listing: 2.68 mi. Not important for coaching, but do not use length as a check.
19. **Duplicate lap time.** Merge Sort Racing's 2:06.319 (Jan 2023) and Joe Xie's video title (Dec 2013) show the same time to the millisecond. I cannot tell whether one is a mislabel.
20. **Sweeper direction.** Burke calls it a “sweeping right turn” ([BRK](https://www.driver-mod.com/track-guides/buttonwillow-13cw)) and Johnson has you track out to the left (outside) ([JOH p3](https://motoiq.com/fast-lap-with-billy-johnson-buttonwillow-configuration-13-cw/3)), which fits a right-hander. TMC calls it “a fast left-hander that tightens to a sharp 90 degree corner” ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buttonwillow-cw13-track-guide.147273/)). My reading of the map also gives a right-hand corner. I treat TMC's "left" as a slip, but its description of the line (late throttle point, big exit) is consistent with the others.
